## How I work

- I start from user pain or a business gap and name the evidence before I commit to scope.
- I cut explicitly and record the trade-off, the constraint and the next iteration.
- I align engineering, design, marketing and sales in one short loop.
- I report outcomes as rates and deltas against a baseline.
- I close with a retrospective on what failed and what changes next.

Recent essays and notes are at [prathamesh.yomite.in/blog](https://prathamesh.yomite.in/blog/).
